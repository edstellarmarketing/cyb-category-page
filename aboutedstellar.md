# About Edstellar: Section Plan

## Purpose
Replace the current CustomersPartners section with a dedicated "About Edstellar" section that answers the single most important question a procurement team, CISO, or L&D leader asks before shortlisting a training provider:

> **"Why should we trust Edstellar with our cybersecurity workforce?"**

This section must do the work of a vendor briefing in a few seconds of scroll. Conviction through proof, not claims.

---

## Buyer Psychology: What Enterprise Decision-Makers Look For

Before choosing a cybersecurity training provider, security leaders and L&D heads evaluate across these dimensions:

| What They Ask | What They Fear | What Converts Them |
|---|---|---|
| Are they credible in our space? | Generic content, no domain depth | Industry body partnerships, recognizable client logos |
| Can they operate at our scale? | Single-country vendors, rigid delivery | Trainer count, country coverage, on-site + virtual |
| Will this be customized or off-the-shelf? | One-size programs that don't fit our stack | Role-based paths, stack-aligned delivery |
| Can we prove ROI to the board? | Training spend with no measurable outcome | Pre/post assessments, skill metrics, MTTD/MTTR improvement |
| How fast can they mobilize? | Long onboarding, slow cohort setup | Vetted trainer network ready to deploy |
| Do they understand our compliance context? | Generic training that ignores our regulatory regime | GDPR, DPDP, PCI-DSS, SOC2, sector-specific alignment |
| Are they a vendor or a partner? | Transactional relationships, no accountability | CoE model, dedicated program management |

---

## Section Goal

Convert a scrolling enterprise buyer into someone who clicks "Partner with Edstellar" or submits the contact form by stacking three things:

1. **A bold positioning statement** that frames Edstellar's unique place in the market
2. **Proof points** with hard numbers and logos that validate the claim
3. **Trust signals** from accreditation partners and global reach that reduce perceived risk

## Proposed Content Structure

### 1. Section Label (eyebrow)
`Why Edstellar`

### 2. Headline

**Selected:**
"Why Edstellar is Trusted by Enterprises to Build Cybersecurity Capability"

### 3. Goal Statement (ally framing, inspired by Cybrary's opener)
> Our goal at Edstellar is to help your security teams shift from reacting to threats to actively owning your organisation's cyber defence capability. Every training program we deliver is built by industry practitioners and mapped to the roles, tools, and compliance frameworks your business runs on.

### 4. Bold Positioning Line (ownable, memorable closer to the opening block)
> We are not just a training provider. We help enterprises build the cybersecurity capability that actually protects the business.

This line closes the opening block before proof pillars begin. It should be set apart typographically — larger, bolder, or with a highlight treatment to make it scannable and memorable.

## Design Direction

- **Background:** `#F8F9FB` (current). Light gray distinguishes from white sections above and below.
- **Layout:** Two-column at desktop (headline + pillars left, map or logo grid right), stacked on mobile.
- **Accreditation strip:** Horizontal logo row, grayscale logos, subtle border above.
- **Client logos:** Grayscale marquee or static grid, 2 to 3 rows, no color to avoid visual noise.
- **Typography:** Consistent with site. Riona Sans Light for body, Riona Sans Bold for stats.
- **CTA placement:** Bottom of section, full-width strip or inline below client logos.
