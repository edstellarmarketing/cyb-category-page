## 1. Competitor white space (positioning)

The 2026 AI-training market splits into two camps; Edstellar owns the gap between them:
- **Self-paced platforms** (DataCamp, Coursera/Udemy, Skillsoft, Go1, Pluralsight) — scalable, but no
  live instruction, no customization, no cohort accountability.
- **Consulting-led one-offs** (Deloitte AI Academy, Correlation One, Xebia) — high-touch but expensive,
  project-scoped, not a repeatable enterprise training program.

**Edstellar's white space:** instructor-led, *customized*, closed-cohort group training, vetted/trial-able
trainers, delivered on-site or virtual across 100+ countries, tied to measurable business outcomes.
