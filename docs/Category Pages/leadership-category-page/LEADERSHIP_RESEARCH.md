## 1. Competitor white space (positioning)

The 2026 leadership-development market splits into three camps; Edstellar owns the gap between them:
- **Self-paced content libraries** (LinkedIn Learning, Coursera, Skillsoft Leadership, FranklinCovey digital,
  Udemy Business) — cheap and scalable, but no live facilitation, no cohort accountability, no behaviour change
  measured back to the business. Leadership is a practised skill; video does not build it.
- **Elite executive education** (Harvard/INSEAD/CCL open programs, Korn Ferry, McKinsey leadership academies) —
  high-credibility but priced per-seat for a handful of senior people, calendar-locked, and rarely customised to
  the client's own competency model or restructure.
- **Generalist L&D vendors** — deliver a fixed catalogue workshop, but cannot field a vetted facilitator for
  *this* leadership tier (first-time manager vs. board) across *this* client's geographies.

**Edstellar's white space:** instructor-led, *customised* to the client's leadership framework and tier,
delivered as a closed cohort, with vetted/trial-able facilitators, on-site or virtual across 100+ countries,
tied to measurable outcomes (engagement, retention, manager-effectiveness and 360 score uplift) a CHRO can
report to the board.
