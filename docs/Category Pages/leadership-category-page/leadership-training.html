Edstellar Approach to Organizational Leadership Training

From discovery to mastery in five steps. A consultative, outcome-led methodology that turns leadership training into measurable capability inside your organisation.

Start with a discovery call
  1. 01

    Discover

    We meet with your CHRO and L&D team to map current leadership capability against your competency model and manager tiers. Output: a prioritised capability-gap report with ROI-aligned objectives.

  2. 02

    Design

    We build a custom curriculum mapped to your competency framework and leader tiers, then shortlist certified facilitators from our 1,500+ network for trial sessions with your own leaders.

  3. 03

    Deliver

    Instructor-led sessions, virtual, on-site or hybrid, in your language, with live simulations, role-plays, action-learning projects and peer-coaching circles built into every cohort.

  4. 04

    Assess

    Pre and post 360-degree assessments, behaviour-change tracking and people KPIs like engagement and retention. Every cohort closes with a measurable capability delta.

  5. 05

    Optimise

    Quarterly retrospectives, refresher modules, coaching follow-through and a dedicated Learning Services Manager driving long-term leadership ROI.

ILT, or Instructor-Led Training, is on-site, in-person delivery with a facilitator at your premises. VILT, or Virtual Instructor-Led Training, is the live online equivalent in real time. Both are practice-based with simulations and role-plays. The choice is location and logistics, not quality.

Every facilitator in Edstellar's 1,500+ network passes multi-stage vetting: a capability assessment, a live delivery evaluation and ongoing client feedback. Facilitators are matched by domain and tier expertise. You can request a trial session with your leaders before committing.

Every cohort runs pre and post 360 assessments to establish a measurable capability delta. Results are shared in a post-program delivery summary and mapped to people KPIs such as engagement, retention and manager-effectiveness scores your CHRO can take to the board.
