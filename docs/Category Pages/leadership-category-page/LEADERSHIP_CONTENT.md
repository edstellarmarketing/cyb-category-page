## 13 — FAQ (10 Qs; heading "Frequently Asked Questions"; answers 200–320 chars)

1. **Target Audience (MANDATORY, first):** Q "Who should attend Edstellar's leadership training programs?" A names ≥5 roles: first-time and frontline managers, team leads and supervisors, mid-level managers, senior managers and directors, VPs and C-suite executives, plus high-potential and succession candidates, with L&D mapping cohorts by tier to upskill or reskill leaders.
2. **Delivery Format:** all programs instructor-led, live, closed-cohort; ILT and VILT named; no self-paced or recorded-only modules.
3. **Customization:** customized around your competency model, manager tiers and business goals; every engagement starts with a discovery call and training-needs analysis.
4. **Group Training & Cohort Size:** 5 trainees to 800+; parallel cohorts across locations; group size does not dilute quality because every session is instructor-led, not self-paced.
5. **ILT vs VILT:** defines both in plain terms; both are live and practice-based with simulations and role-plays; the choice is location and logistics, not quality.
6. **Facilitator Vetting:** multi-stage (capability assessment, delivery evaluation, client feedback); 1,500+ network; trial session with your leaders before commitment.
7. **Assessment & Outcomes:** pre/post 360 assessments produce a measurable capability delta; mapped to people KPIs like engagement, retention and manager-effectiveness scores.
8. **Industries:** ≥6 industries; programs adapted to each sector's leadership context and operating model; delivered across 100+ countries.
9. **Vendor Selection (high-intent):** "How do we select the right leadership corporate training provider?" — criteria: instructor-led delivery, customization to your competency model, vetted facilitators, measurable outcomes, and a trial session before you commit. (2nd and final use of "corporate training provider" — K7 ceiling respected.)
10. **Group vs Individual:** "Why is group training more effective for leaders than individual courses?" — shared organisational context, peer learning across the cohort, a common leadership language, and measurable team-level outcomes individual licenses cannot deliver.
