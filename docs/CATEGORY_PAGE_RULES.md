# Category Page Rules

Rules for writing content on any new Edstellar training category page. Every section has mandatory keywords and USP requirements. Read this before writing a single word of copy.

---

## Core USPs — Must Appear on Every Category Page

These are Edstellar's primary differentiators and must be present, visible, and prominent on every category page:

| USP | Priority | Required Phrase Form |
|---|---|---|
| **Instructor-led training** | #1 — primary USP | "instructor-led training", "instructor-led [category] training", "instructor-led group training" |
| **Customized instructor-led training programs** | #2 — primary USP | "customized instructor-led training", "customized [category] training programs" |
| **Group training** | #3 | "group training", "[category] group training programs" |
| Upskilling | Required | "upskill employees", "upskilling security teams" |
| Reskilling | Required | "reskill", "reskilling lateral movers", "reskilling employees" |
| Skilling | Required | "skilling new hires", "entry-level skilling" |
| Employee training | Required | "employee training", "employee [category] training programs" |

> Instructor-led is the single most important keyword and differentiator for Edstellar. It must appear in the HeroSlider, WelcomeStrip H1, and at minimum two additional sections on every page. Never let a section description go past two sentences without anchoring back to instructor-led delivery.

---

## Writing Rules (Apply to All Sections)

- **No eyebrow headings.** Do not place a small uppercase label above any section heading. Section headings must stand alone and carry the section identity without an eyebrow. The only exception is the slide category label inside HeroSlider, which is scoped to the hero slide UI and is not a page-section eyebrow.
- **No em-dashes** anywhere on the page. Use a period, comma, or colon instead.
- **No "course"** outside of FAQs. Always use "program", "training program", or "training programs".
- **Edstellar is not an accreditation body.** Never write "certified by Edstellar", "Edstellar-certified", or anything that implies Edstellar grants industry certifications.
- **No URL guessing.** CTAs must link to `#contact`, `#catalog`, or a confirmed URL only.
- **No em-dashes.** Repeated because it keeps happening.

## Section-by-Section Keyword Rules

### 1. HeroSlider

**Purpose:** First impression. Sets category authority and USP positioning.

**Mandatory keywords (every slide):**
- `instructor-led` — must appear in body copy of slide 1 (the primary/corporate slide)
- `upskill` and `reskill` — use together: "upskill and reskill employees"
- `group training` — at least one slide must say "[category] group training"
- `employees` — the person being trained is always an employee, not a generic "learner"

**Slide 1 (Corporate/Primary) — required elements:**
- Slide category label (inside hero UI only, not an eyebrow heading): "Corporate [Category] Training"
- Description must contain: `instructor-led`, `upskill and reskill employees`, `group training`, `200+ programs` (or category-specific count), `measurable outcomes`

**Industry slides (slides 2–4):**
- Each must contain `instructor-led` OR `customized` in the description
- Each must include relevant compliance or regulation terms for that industry vertical
- At least two of the four slides must use "reskill" or "reskilling"

**Prohibited:**
- Do not use "course" — use "program" or "training program"
- Do not say "online training" — Edstellar delivers instructor-led, not self-paced online

### 2. WelcomeStrip (H1 + Overview Section)

**Purpose:** SEO anchor, content authority, five-part framework introduction.

**H1 — required structure:**
```
Customized [Category] Training Programs for Enterprise teams
```
The word **Customized** must open the H1. This establishes customized instructor-led training as the primary value proposition from the very first heading a search engine and visitor reads.

**Paragraph 1 — required keywords:**
- `skilling` — entry-level skilling or new hire skilling
- `upskilling` — role-specific upskilling
- `reskilling` — senior-level or lateral reskilling
- `group training cohort` — every group training cohort
- `vendor-certified trainers`

**Paragraph 2 — required keywords:**
- `customized instructor-led training` — **must appear in bold or otherwise marked as a highlight** in the rendered page, as it is Edstellar's primary USP
- `employee training` — "turns employee training into measurable outcomes"
- `on-site` and `virtual` delivery options

**Bullet points (three required bullets):**
1. Strategy-led design — capability roadmap, security/domain goals
2. Hands-on by default — labs, real-world practice environment
3. Measurable ROI — pre/post assessments, KPIs, skill improvement rates

**Prohibited:**
- Do not use generic phrases like "learn from experts" — too weak. Use "matched to vetted, vendor-certified trainers"
- Do not say "online" or "e-learning"

## Instructor-led Highlighting Rule

> In the WelcomeStrip paragraph 2, the phrase **"customized instructor-led training"** must be rendered in bold (`<strong>`) or marked with a visual highlight. This is Edstellar's primary USP and it must not sit as plain body text in the most-read overview section of the page.

Implementation in JSX:
```tsx
<p>
  Every{" "}
  <strong style={{ color: "#1B1D52" }}>customized instructor-led training</strong>{" "}
  program is delivered onsite, off-site or virtually...
</p>
```

This same treatment should be considered for the HeroSlider description when the layout allows it.

## Competitor Differentiation Checklist

Before publishing a new category page, verify the copy answers these questions that SANS, OffSec, EC-Council, Cybrary, and Infosec Institute do NOT answer clearly:

- [ ] Does the page say Edstellar **builds** the program (not sells a catalog seat)?
- [ ] Does the page mention **trainer vetting** (not just "expert trainers")?
- [ ] Does the page say **on-site or virtual** (not only online / virtual)?
- [ ] Does the page reference **local compliance** or in-region delivery?
- [ ] Does the page speak to **L&D language** (skilling, upskilling, reskilling, workforce development) — not just "get certified"?
- [ ] Does the page mention **measurable skill outcomes** (not just "pass the exam")?
- [ ] Does the page cover both **small group** and **large enterprise** rollouts?
